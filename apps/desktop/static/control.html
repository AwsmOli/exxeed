          Welcome to Exxeed

          A track coach for iRacing: it talks you round a new circuit while you drive. Four things to know.

          1. Run iRacing in borderless windowed, not fullscreen. Overlays cannot draw over exclusive fullscreen. In iRacing: Options → Graphics → Display, and choose borderless window. This is the setting most overlay problems come down to.
          2. Get callouts for your track. Install a pack another driver made in Content, or turn a YouTube track guide into callouts with Import from YouTube in Track Coach. Callouts play in your headset when you drive that track.
          3. Arrange your overlays. In Overlays, tick the ones you want and press Edit to drag and size them over the sim. The gear beside an overlay chooses what it shows. Overlays only show while iRacing is the window in front.
          4. Try it now. Test mode drives a lap through the overlays and callouts without the sim.

          Edit theme

          Changes show on the open overlays as you type. theme.json sets colours and shapes; theme.css restyles anything; a template rebuilds an overlay. Turn on Test mode, or Edit on a profile, to have overlays to look at.
